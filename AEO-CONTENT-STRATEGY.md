# 🤖 Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Content Strategy

## 🎯 **AEO vs Traditional SEO**

| Traditional SEO | AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) |
|----------------|----------------------------------|
| Optimizes for search rankings | Optimizes for AI answer engines |
| Focus on keyword density | Focus on comprehensive answers |
| 10 blue links format | Direct answers & conversational |
| Backlinks important | Content quality & structure crucial |

### **Content Structure for AEO**
```markdown
# Title: Question-based or Solution-focused

## Quick Answer (Featured Snippet)
- Direct answer in first 50-60 words
- Use structured format (numbered lists, bullet points)

## Comprehensive Deep-dive
- Detailed explanation
- Multiple H2, H3 headings as questions
- Tables, charts, statistics
- Examples and case studies

## FAQ Section
- 5-10 related questions
- JSON-LD FAQ schema

## Related Topics & Internal Links
- Link to relevant service pages
- Cross-link related blog posts
```
